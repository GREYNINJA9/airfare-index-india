Index Methodology & Econometric Formulations

Mathematical principles adhering to ILO, IMF, and MoSPI Consumer Price Index Manuals

1. Laspeyres Price Index (Official MoSPI Standard)

Formula
ILt = [ Σ (Pi,t · Qi,0) / Σ (Pi,0 · Qi,0) ] × 100

Measures the change in the total expenditure required to purchase a fixed basket of domestic flight routes established in base year 2024. Weights Qi,0 represent annual DGCA passenger traffic volume on each corridor.

2. Weighted Geometric Jevons Price Index

Formula
IJt = ∏ (Pi,t / Pi,0)wi × 100

Calculates the geometric weighted mean of price relatives. Satisfies the time-reversal and circularity axioms, preventing the upward substitution bias inherent in fixed-basket Laspeyres aggregations.

3. Advance Booking Decay Weighting Scheme

Formula
W(d) = exp( - λ · d ),   where λ = 0.038

Empirical booking volume weights decay exponentially as lead time increases, reflecting that over 58% of domestic tickets in India are purchased within 14 days of departure.

4. Two-Stage Outlier & Noise Rejection Filter

Algorithm
[ Q1 - 1.5 · IQR,   Q3 + 1.5 · IQR ] ∩ [ μ ± 3σ ]

Every raw scraped fare quote is passed through both an interquartile range (IQR) check and a 3-standard-deviation filter per route-carrier cell, eliminating web scraping glitches, zero prices, and abnormal first-class fares.
